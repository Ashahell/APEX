Governance Cadence
================
- Quarterly governance reviews with security, architecture, and product leadership.
- Bi-weekly crosswalks for OpenClaw/AgentZero/Hermes/OpenFang parity.
- Monthly audit trail reviews and runbook health checks.
- Change-control protocol for security posture, CI gates, and deployment procedures.
- Sign-off criteria: cadence adherence, risk register updates, and updated runbooks.
